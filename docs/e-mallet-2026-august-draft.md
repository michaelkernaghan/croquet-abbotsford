## 10. Official name of the club

- **Legal entity:** Fraser Valley Croquet Society (BC Society S0075824, registered 2023)
- **Public-facing brand at Mill Lake:** Abbotsford Croquet Club
- **Host club:** Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club, Mill Lake Park
- Website: https://abbotsfordcroquet.com
- Facebook: Fraser Valley Croquet Society

## 1. History of the club

The Fraser Valley Croquet Society was registered as a BC non-profit in 2023,
with its founding home in Mission. It expanded to Abbotsford the following
year, operating under the public-facing name **Abbotsford Croquet Club**
at Mill Lake Park. The society remains the legal entity for regional
operations, municipal partnerships, and Mission-area programming; the
Abbotsford Croquet Club is the operating program at Mill Lake.

In 2026 the society is also returning to Mission with a six-week
*Introduction to Croquet for Adults and Seniors* course at the Mission
Leisure Centre — the community where it all started.

## 2. When was croquet introduced, how, and by whom?

- **Introduced at Mill Lake:** 2024
- **By whom:** Michael Kernaghan, Director, Fraser Valley Croquet Society,
  in partnership with the Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club.
- **How:** The lawn bowling club was looking for new members and additional
  summer use for its green; the croquet society was looking for a mowed
  surface, working sprinklers, and a welcoming host. A partnership was struck
  — the same Victoria-style shared-greens model that has worked for over a
  decade on Vancouver Island.

## 3. How many bowlers (at the host lawn bowling club)?

**~30 members** at Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club.

## 4. How many croquet players?

**7 active croquet members** in 2026.

## 5. Annual events

- **Open Day for potential new members** — the club's main annual event,
  held each spring. Borrowed mallets, patient instruction, and the standard
  invitation to come once and stay for tea.
- A **Golf Croquet club tournament** was held at Mill Lake on May 31, 2025.
  A second tournament in September 2026 is under consideration.

## 6. How often do you play?

- **Tuesday and Thursday evenings, 6:00 PM** — April through September
- **Selected Saturdays** for the Open Day and tournament play
- That's **two regular sessions a week** across the season.

## 7. Format — AC, GC, or Snake?

**Mostly Golf Croquet** for club play and tournaments.
**Association Croquet** is pursued by individual members in their own practice.

## 8. How many greens?

**One green**, shared with the lawn bowlers. The two clubs co-exist on the
same surface — bowlers on their schedule, croquet players on theirs — with
no friction beyond the occasional polite negotiation over who gets the
better-rolling end.

## 11. Equipment

The club has invested in tournament-grade kit:

- **Hoops:** Foxy
- **Balls:** Invictus, with Sunshiny as spares
- **Mallets:**
  - Introductory club mallets from **Oakley Woods** (Canadian-made)
  - Several members own **Predator** mallets
  - One member plays a **Trimmer**

## 12. Social or competitive?

**Both, with a social tilt.**
- Regular Tuesday/Thursday evenings are social and welcoming — newcomers
  encouraged, instruction freely given, tea afterwards.
- The May 2025 Golf Croquet tournament and the annual Open Day give the
  competitive and outreach sides of the club a structured outlet.
- In June 2026 a club member won the A Flight at the Croquet Canada
  National Championship in Bayfield, Ontario.

## 13. Court size

**Full size** (35 × 28 yards).
