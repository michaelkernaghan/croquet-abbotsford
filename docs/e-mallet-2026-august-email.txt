OFFICIAL NAME

The legal entity is the Fraser Valley Croquet Society, registered as a
BC non-profit in 2023 (Society S0075824). Our public-facing brand at
Mill Lake Park is the Abbotsford Croquet Club, and we operate as
guests of the Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club.

Website: https://abbotsfordcroquet.com
Facebook: Fraser Valley Croquet Society


HISTORY OF THE CLUB

The Fraser Valley Croquet Society was founded in 2023 with its
original home in Mission, BC. It expanded to Abbotsford the following
year, operating as the Abbotsford Croquet Club at Mill Lake Park.
The society remains the legal entity for regional operations and
municipal partnerships, while the Abbotsford Croquet Club is the
operating program at Mill Lake. In 2026 the society is also returning
to Mission with a six-week Introduction to Croquet for Adults and
Seniors course at the Mission Leisure Centre - the community where
it all started.


WHEN AND HOW CROQUET WAS INTRODUCED

Croquet arrived at Mill Lake in 2024, introduced by Michael Kernaghan,
Director of the Fraser Valley Croquet Society, in partnership with the
Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club. The lawn bowling club was looking for
new members and additional summer use for its green; the croquet
society was looking for a mowed surface, working sprinklers, and a
welcoming host. A partnership was struck - the same shared-greens
model that has worked on Vancouver Island for over a decade.


HOW MANY BOWLERS

About 30 members at the Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club.


HOW MANY CROQUET PLAYERS

Seven active croquet members in 2026.


ANNUAL EVENTS

The club's main annual event is the spring Open Day for potential new
members - borrowed mallets, patient instruction, and the standard
invitation to come once and stay for tea. A Golf Croquet club
tournament was held at Mill Lake on May 31, 2025, and a second
tournament in September 2026 is under consideration.


HOW OFTEN WE PLAY

Tuesday and Thursday evenings at 6:00 PM, April through September, plus
selected Saturdays for the Open Day and tournament play. Two regular
sessions a week across the season.


FORMAT

Mostly Golf Croquet for club play and tournaments. Association
Croquet is pursued by individual members in their own practice.


HOW MANY GREENS

One green, shared with the lawn bowlers. The two clubs co-exist on the
same surface - bowlers on their schedule, croquet players on theirs -
with no friction beyond the occasional polite negotiation over who
gets the better-rolling end.


EQUIPMENT

The club has invested in tournament-grade kit:

- Hoops: Foxy
- Balls: Invictus, with Sunshiny as spares
- Mallets: introductory club mallets from Oakley Woods (Canadian-made);
  several members own Predator mallets, and one member plays a Trimmer.


SOCIAL OR COMPETITIVE

Both, with a social tilt. Regular Tuesday and Thursday evenings are
social and welcoming - newcomers encouraged, instruction freely given,
tea afterwards. The May 2025 Golf Croquet tournament and the annual
Open Day give the competitive and outreach sides of the club a
structured outlet. In June 2026 a club member won the A Flight at the
Croquet Canada National Championship in Bayfield, Ontario.
