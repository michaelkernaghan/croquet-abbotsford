Please find attached my submission regarding the Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy currently open for public feedback.

As Director of the Abbotsford Croquet Club, I am requesting that croquet be included in the strategy alongside other lawn sports such as lawn bowling. The attached submission outlines the rationale for including croquet and provides specific requests for consideration during the Stage 4 finalization process.

Thank you for the opportunity to provide feedback on this important long-term planning initiative.
