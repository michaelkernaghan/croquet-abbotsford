# Submission: Include Croquet in Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy

**To:** Cory Nesci, Planning Manager
**Email:** fieldsandcourts@abbotsford.ca
**Re:** Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy - Public Feedback
**Date:** February 26, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

I am writing to request that **croquet** be explicitly included in the City of Abbotsford's Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy. While lawn bowling, bocce, and horseshoe pitches are currently recognized under "Other outdoor sport infrastructure," croquet - a similar lawn sport with an active local presence - is notably absent from the strategy.

**Importantly, the Abbotsford Croquet Club currently shares facilities with the Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club.** This means croquet is already part of the city's sport infrastructure, yet remains unrecognized in the long-term planning strategy.

## Background: Croquet in Abbotsford

The Abbotsford Croquet Club is an established organization that has been serving the community for years. **The club currently shares facilities with the Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club**, demonstrating successful multi-use of city sport infrastructure.

Croquet is:

- **An organized sport** with regular club play, tournaments, and competitive opportunities
- **Already using city facilities** - the shared lawn bowling/croquet facility represents existing city investment
- **Growing in the region** - part of the Pacific Northwest croquet circuit including clubs in Victoria, Vancouver, Seattle, and Portland
- **Accessible to diverse populations** - appeals to adults of all ages, requires minimal equipment, and has low barriers to entry
- **Social and strategic** - combines physical activity with tactical thinking and community building

## Why Croquet Deserves Recognition

### 1. Consistency with Current Strategy
The strategy already includes lawn bowling under "Other outdoor sport infrastructure." **The Abbotsford Croquet Club currently shares the same facilities with the Lawn Bowling Club**, yet only lawn bowling is recognized in the strategy. This omission is inconsistent because:

- **Same facilities** - croquet uses the same city-maintained grass surfaces as lawn bowling
- **Same infrastructure** - the city is already supporting croquet through its lawn bowling facility investment
- **Comparable demographics** - both sports appeal to older adults and retirees
- **Similar community benefits** - social connection, outdoor recreation, accessible physical activity
- **Proven compatibility** - the existing shared-use arrangement demonstrates successful multi-sport facility management

### 2. Alignment with City Goals
Including croquet supports the strategy's stated objectives:
- **Accommodating population growth** - particularly the aging demographic expected over the next 15 years
- **Improving facility management** - croquet can share spaces with other lawn sports
- **Collaborative opportunities** - potential partnerships with the Abbotsford Croquet Club for programming and maintenance

### 3. Infrastructure Already in Place
**The city is already supporting croquet through the shared lawn bowling facility.** This means:
- **Zero additional infrastructure cost** - the facility already exists and serves both sports
- **Proven multi-use model** - the current arrangement demonstrates effective facility sharing
- **Efficient use of city investment** - recognizing croquet acknowledges the full value of existing infrastructure
- **Scalability** - the successful shared-use model could be replicated in future facility planning

### 4. Regional and Competitive Context
The Pacific Northwest has an active croquet network. Abbotsford's recognition of croquet in its long-term strategy would:
- Position the city as a regional destination for tournaments
- Attract visitors for multi-day events
- Support local players competing at provincial and national levels

## Specific Requests

I respectfully request that the City:

1. **Add croquet** to the list of sports explicitly recognized under "Other outdoor sport infrastructure"
2. **Include croquet facilities** in the inventory assessment of current and potential future sites
3. **Consult with the Abbotsford Croquet Club** during Stage 4 finalization to understand facility needs and opportunities
4. **Consider multi-use lawn sport areas** that can accommodate croquet, lawn bowling, and bocce to maximize facility utilization

## Conclusion

The omission of croquet from the Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy appears to be an oversight rather than a deliberate exclusion. **Croquet is already part of Abbotsford's sport infrastructure through the shared lawn bowling facility** - the strategy simply fails to recognize it.

As the City plans for the next 15+ years of sport infrastructure, formally recognizing croquet alongside lawn bowling will:
- **Accurately reflect current facility use** - the strategy should document what's already happening
- **Ensure comprehensive planning** for diverse recreational needs
- **Support an existing and growing local club** that's already using city facilities
- **Validate the multi-use model** that's proven successful at the lawn bowling/croquet facility
- **Maximize the value of grass field investments** by acknowledging all user groups

I am happy to provide additional information about croquet's local presence, facility requirements, or community benefits. Thank you for considering this feedback as you finalize the Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy.
