# Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy Submission

## Overview
Submission to the City of Abbotsford requesting that croquet be included in the 15+ year Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy.

## Key Points
- **Issue**: Lawn bowling is recognized in the strategy, but croquet is not
- **Current situation**: Abbotsford Croquet Club shares facilities with Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club
- **Argument**: Croquet already uses city infrastructure but is not recognized in long-term planning

## Timeline

## Requested Actions
1. Add croquet to recognized sports under "Other outdoor sport infrastructure"
2. Include croquet facilities in inventory assessment
3. Consult with Abbotsford Croquet Club during Stage 4
4. Consider multi-use lawn sport areas (croquet/lawn bowling/bocce)
