# Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy Submission

## Overview
Submission to the City of Abbotsford requesting that croquet be included in the 15+ year Sport Field and Sport Court Strategy.

## Key Points
- **Issue**: Lawn bowling is recognized in the strategy, but croquet is not
- **Current situation**: Abbotsford Croquet Club shares facilities with Abbotsford Lawn Bowling Club
- **Argument**: Croquet already uses city infrastructure but is not recognized in long-term planning

## Requested Actions
1. Add croquet to recognized sports under "Other outdoor sport infrastructure"
2. Include croquet facilities in inventory assessment
3. Consult with Abbotsford Croquet Club during Stage 4
4. Consider multi-use lawn sport areas (croquet/lawn bowling/bocce)

## Key Findings from City Documents (reviewed Feb 26, 2026)

### Lawn Bowling Facility Relocation (Draft Strategy Section 3.4, Action B, p.56)
- Mill Lake Park Master Plan proposes relocating the lawn bowling facility off-site
- The north ball diamond will also be removed
- Mill Lake is the only lawn bowling facility in the city
- City will look for existing sport field/court sites suitable for a new lawn bowling green
- Criteria: underutilized site, suitable soil/irrigation/grading, existing support infrastructure
- Only the Lawn Bowling Club is named for engagement -- Croquet Club is not mentioned
- This directly impacts the Croquet Club since we share the facility

### Croquet Status in Strategy
- Croquet is completely absent from both documents (not in sports list, inventory, or draft actions)
- Sports covered: sport fields, ball diamonds, games courts, pickleball, tennis, sand volleyball, skate parks, bike parks, disc golf, lawn bowling, horseshoes
- Current inventory shows "1 Lawn Bowling Green" -- no croquet mentioned
- SWOC Opportunities mention growth of "pickleball, wheeled sports, and cricket" -- no croquet
