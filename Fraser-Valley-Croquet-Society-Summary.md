## 5. Organizational Structure

**Relationship between entities:**
- **Fraser Valley Croquet Society** = Regional BC registered non-profit society
- **Abbotsford Croquet Club** = Operating program/brand within the society
- **Mission Programs** = New initiative returning to society's founding community

The society uses "Abbotsford Croquet Club" as its public-facing brand for the Mill Lake Park programs, while maintaining "Fraser Valley Croquet Society" as the official legal entity for regional operations, formal applications, and municipal partnerships.
