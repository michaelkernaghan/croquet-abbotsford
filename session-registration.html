Join a Session

We play Tuesdays and Thursdays at 6:00 PM from April to September at Mill Lake Park, with room for about eight players a session. Mallets and balls are provided and no experience is needed - beginners are genuinely welcome, and someone will show you how to play.

Let us know you are coming

Email us and we will look out for you on the day. Tell us which evening suits you and whether you have played before.

abbotsfordcroquet@gmail.com

Prefer to just turn up? That is fine too. Arrive a few minutes before 6:00 and introduce yourself.

Upcoming sessions

  • Tuesdays and Thursdays at 6:00 PM, April to September.

What to bring

  • Flat-soled shoes, to protect the lawn - no heels or spikes
  • Something for the weather; we play in light rain but not heavy
  • Nothing else - mallets, balls and hoops are all provided
