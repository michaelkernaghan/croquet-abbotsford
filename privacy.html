Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

The short version. We are a volunteer croquet club, not a business. This site sets no cookies, shows no advertising, and we do not sell or share member information with anyone. The only personal details we hold are the ones you type into the session registration form.

What we collect when you register for a session

The session registration form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number, which session you want, your experience level, and any comments you choose to add. You give us those details deliberately, and we use them for one thing: organising the session you signed up for and getting back to you about it.

Submissions are handled by Netlify, the company that hosts this website, and are forwarded to the club's email address. They are visible to the club volunteers who run the sessions. We do not add you to any mailing list without asking, and we do not pass your details to any other organisation.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see roughly how many people visit the site and which pages they read. It was chosen specifically because it is privacy-preserving: it sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device, does not follow you to other websites, and does not build a profile of you. We see aggregate counts, never individuals.

This is also why the site has no cookie banner. There is nothing to consent to, because nothing is being stored on your device or tracked across sites.

Fonts and embedded content

Some pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google receives a request from your browser for those font files. Some pages also embed videos or link out to other organisations; once you follow a link or play an embedded video, that site's own privacy policy applies, not ours.

How long we keep things

Session registrations are kept for the season they relate to and are cleared out afterwards. Visitor statistics are aggregate counts with nothing personal in them to keep.

Asking us to delete your details

Email abbotsfordcroquet@gmail.com and ask. We will remove what we hold about you and confirm when it is done. You are also welcome to ask what we hold in the first place.
